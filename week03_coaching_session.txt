<!-- This runs the loop silently -->
<% projects.forEach(project => { %>

    <!-- This prints the project title on the page -->
    <p><%= project.title %></p>

<% }) %>

So <% %> is the engine working behind the scenes, and <%= %> is what shows up on screen.
